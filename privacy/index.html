Privacy policy

Last updated 26 November 2026

This policy explains what FuelR collects, why, who processes it, and how you can delete it. It applies to the FuelR app on Android and iOS.

1. What we collect

  • Account: your name, email address and a user ID.
  • Vehicles: type, make, model, year, fuel type, and optional details like registration number, odometer and tank size.
  • Fills: amount, litres, price, fuel grade, odometer, date and the pump you filled at.
  • Precise location: only while you use the map or add a fill, to show nearby pumps and identify the one you’re at.
  • Photos: pump-display or dashboard photos you choose to take or upload.
  • Diagnostics: crash logs and device identifiers collected by the Google Maps SDK.

2. How we use it

To run FuelR’s features — logging fills, calculating mileage and cost, finding and comparing pumps, and showing pumps on the map — and to keep your account secure. We don’t sell your data, show ads, or use it for profiling.

3. Who processes it

  • Cloud database provider: stores your account and app data securely, including dashboard photos, which are kept privately with your vehicle.
  • Google Maps Platform: shows the map and finds nearby pumps. It receives your location when you use the map or add a fill. Google’s Privacy Policy applies to this.
  • AI service provider: reads the numbers from the pump-display and dashboard photos you take. Pump-display photos aren’t kept; dashboard photos are stored privately with your vehicle. Your photos are not used to train their models.

These providers process your data only to run FuelR’s features, never for their own purposes.

4. Location

Location is optional. FuelR uses it only while the app is open — on the map and when you add a fill. It never tracks you in the background and never shares your location with other users. Without location you can still use FuelR and pick pumps manually.

5. Storage & security

Data is encrypted in transit (HTTPS) and protected by account-level access rules so only you can see your records.

6. Deleting your data

Delete your account any time from Settings → Delete account. This permanently removes your account, vehicles, fills and photos. Pumps you added to the shared map stay available to other users, with no link to your account. You can also email agrawal.abhinav180@gmail.com from your registered address and we’ll delete it within 30 days.

7. Retention

We keep your data while your account is active. After deletion, it is removed from live systems immediately and from backups within 30 days.

8. Your rights

Under India’s Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 you can access, correct or erase your data, withdraw consent, and raise a grievance. Most of this you can do in the app; for anything else, email us.

9. Children

FuelR is for people 18 and older. We don’t knowingly collect data from children.

10. Changes

If we change this policy in a meaningful way, we’ll tell you in the app before it takes effect.

11. Contact & grievances

Questions, requests or complaints: agrawal.abhinav180@gmail.com. We reply within 7 days.
