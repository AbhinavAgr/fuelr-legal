Delete your FuelR account

Last updated 25 November 2026

You can delete your FuelR account and its data at any time, in the app or by email.

In the app

Go to Settings → Delete account, then confirm. Your account and data are deleted straight away.

Without the app

Email agrawal.abhinav180@gmail.com from the email address you registered with, with the subject “Delete my FuelR account”. We’ll delete your account within 30 days and reply to confirm.

What is deleted

  • Your account: name, email address and login.
  • Your vehicles and their details.
  • Your fills: amounts, litres, prices, odometer readings and the pumps you visited.
  • Your pump-display and dashboard photos.
  • Your mileage history and insights.

What is kept

Pumps you added to the shared map stay available to other users, with no link to your account. Nothing else is kept.

How long it takes

Deleting in the app removes your data from our live systems immediately. Email requests are handled within 30 days. Backups are cleared within 30 days of deletion.
